  • 2026-07-18 FIX
    Accounting reconciled to Alpaca — dashboard now shows real paper equity; per-close real-fill booking + ledger re-derivation prevent drift
    The dashboard's headline net P&L now shows the REAL Alpaca paper-account equity (+$182.18 on a $100,000 paper base) instead of a sum of the internal trade records, which had drifted out of sync with the broker. It's clearly labelled 'Paper account (simulated)' so it's never mistaken for real cash — a separate real-cash panel comes when the strategy goes live. WHAT WAS WRONG: two layers of drift. (1) The account ledger kept a running realizedPnl counter that was never re-derived when records were corrected, so a paper trade (MCD) that was removed from the records left a phantom -$78.14 in the total, plus an inflated trade count. (2) Each close booked a SIMULATED price — TP1 at the RSI-50 bar close, TP2 at the historical SMA-touch price — not the actual Alpaca fill, so records that closed days later (e.g. runners sold weeks after the SMA touch) understated the real profit. THE FIX: a read-only reconciliation tool (reconcile-account.js, run in the cloud) matched every closed position to its real Alpaca fills, reconstructed the dropped MCD paper trade from the broker's own fill history, and re-derived the ledger — reconciling the paper books to the paper account to the dollar (+$182.37 internal vs +$182.18 Alpaca, a $0.19 fees/rounding residual). Going forward the bot books each close at the CONFIRMED Alpaca fill price (TP1, TP2, and externally-detected closes), stamps each record with whether the price came from a real fill or an estimate, and re-derives the ledger total from the records every time (never a running counter) — so a corrected or removed record can never leave a phantom again. Paper and live books are kept strictly separate: when the strategy goes live a fresh live ledger starts and paper history never mixes into it or into the live tax report. NO strategy rule changed — the RSI-50 TP1 trigger, the SMA50/200/break-even/timeout TP2 exits, the share math, the cancel-first stop logic and the entry/scan logic are all byte-for-byte unchanged; only how the exit PRICE is sourced and how the ledger total is derived changed.   • 2026-07-17 RELEASE
    V2.4.0 PDT-SAFE EXECUTION MODE — a PDT-immune option for accounts under $25,000, chosen at install
    NEW OPT-IN EXECUTION MODE (not a signal-logic change). The standard bot places real broker GTC stops plus a resting intraday short-TP1 limit from the moment of entry — great for tight, intraday fills, but those broker orders CAN fill the SAME day as entry. A same-day round trip is a 'day trade', and a MARGIN account under $25,000 making 4+ day trades in 5 business days gets flagged as a Pattern Day Trader (PDT) by FINRA/Alpaca and can be RESTRICTED from day trading. SID takes SHORT trades, and shorting requires a margin account — so a sub-$25K SID account is PDT-subject. v2.4.0 restores the old v2.0 PDT-immune design as an OPT-IN mode. Set SID_PDT_SAFE=true and the bot places NO broker order that can fire intraday: it skips the resting GTC stop, skips the resting intraday short-TP1 limit, and skips the entry-time broker stop — for BOTH the normal entry path and the Telegram-approved entry path. PDT-safe positions are then managed SOLELY by the once-a-day poll (TP1 at RSI-50, TP2 on the 50/200-day SMA, break-even, or the 30-day timeout, and a stop breach → market close at the NEXT day's open). Because the daily poll only ever acts a day or more after entry and there are no broker orders at the exchange, no position can open and close on the same calendar day → it cannot trigger the PDT rule, so a sub-$25K account won't get locked out. HONEST TRADE-OFFS (stated plainly, they're inherent to being PDT-immune): there is no intraday stop, so a hard mid-day move against you isn't cut until the next run's open (gap/slippage), and you should expect a MODEST hit to profitability — on the order of a few percent — versus the standard PDT version; also a missed scheduled run leaves a position unmanaged for longer (no broker safety-net order). DEFAULT is FALSE = the standard PDT version (unchanged), so nothing changes for existing $25K+ setups. The fresh installer (SID-DEPLOY-PROMPT.md) now ASKS your funding size and recommends the mode: $25,000+ → PDT version (best fills, but you must keep the account above $25,000 at all times); under $25,000 → PDT-safe mode. This is not financial advice — you are responsible for your account type and Alpaca's/FINRA's rules. NO canon rule change — RSI 30/70, MACD alignment, the RSI-50 TP1 trigger, the earnings blackout and the AUTO-tier 80-ticker universe are all untouched, so the headline backtest numbers are unchanged. New: CONFIG.pdtSafe (env SID_PDT_SAFE) + executor.openEntryNoStop(); sid.yml + sid-approve-trade.yml pass SID_PDT_SAFE through.

    V2.2.6 TP2 CANCEL-FIRST FIX — TP2 runner-close no longer fails silently; position cap raised 3→5; BITF removed
    LIVE BUG FIXED — the exact twin of the v2.2.5 TP1 bug, on the TP2 branch. After TP1 banks, the v2.2 broker design leaves a full-RUNNER break-even GTC stop resting on Alpaca, and Alpaca 'holds' the runner shares against it — so when checkPositions Branch B fired a TP2 exit and called the full close (DELETE /v2/positions/SYM), it returned `insufficient qty available for order (requested: N, available: 0)` on EVERY run. The old catch just logged tp2_close_fail and re-queued the position, so it retried silently: PYPL (12-share runner, BE $41.41) failed 4x and ADBE (2-share runner, BE $200.79) 2x over 2026-07-13 to 07-16. IMPORTANT — the TP2 trigger itself was LEGITIMATE, not a false positive: both runners had rallied FAVOURABLY up through their 50-day SMA (PYPL last close $56.73 vs SMA50 $44.51 & SMA200 $52.90; ADBE last close $235.31 vs SMA50 $230.94), so the SMA50-touch TP2 fired correctly — they were stuck only because the CLOSE couldn't land while the BE stop held the shares. FIX (mirrors the v2.2.5 TP1 order of operations): (1) CANCEL the resting BE stop first (+ any lingering -stop) and wait for zero open orders to release the runner shares, (2) submit the runner close and POLL that it fills, (3) TP2 fully closes the runner so no re-stop on success — but on a rejected or unconfirmed close the runner is re-protected with a stop, tp2Hit is NOT booked (so it retries next run), and a LOUD Telegram alarm fires (tg.alertTp2CloseFailed).
