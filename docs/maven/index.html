What Maven is

A 4-hour, long-biased trend-pullback strategy on crypto. It only buys a coin once its trend has demonstrably turned up, then enters the first shallow pullback — banking half early, riding the rest. Longs only. A curated watchlist of Bitcoin-outperforming alts.

The trend gates (a trade is only valid when ALL hold)

① Cross gate — the 4h 20 SMA is above the 200 SMA (past a golden cross). Kills the "falling-knife" longs.

② Gap filter — the 20 sits ≥3% above the 200, skipping the choppy cross/transition zone.

③ MTF 200-slope — both the 4h and daily 200 SMA are rising. No macro uptrend = no trade.

The trade

Entry: after the gates open, 4h RSI dips to its ~35 floor then turns back up (+ MACD turning, reclaim of the 20 SMA) → enter next 4h open.

TP1: close 50% at RSI 50, move the stop to break-even.

Runner: exit when price closes back below the 20 SMA. Time stop: 20 × 4h bars.

Risk: 2% per trade, max 3 concurrent, 3× leverage cap, compounding from $1,000.

Watchlist — curated by relative strength

A coin earns a slot when it's outperforming Bitcoin on the trend (the ALT/BTC ratio rising) — Maven wants the leaders. Reviewed periodically; coins that lose strength are dropped.

Backtest (portfolio sim, 2023–2026, curated watchlist): ~+60% on $1,000, ~4.6% max drawdown, ~70% win rate, PF ~4.4. Backtested — not a forecast.
